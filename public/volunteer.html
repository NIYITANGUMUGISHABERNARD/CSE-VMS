Welcome to the CSE-VMS Volunteer Panel

As a Volunteer, you are an important link between community needs and practical community support. Your role includes maintaining your profile, registering your skills, reviewing assignments, communicating with the Coordinator, supporting Community Members, participating in community activities, and providing feedback.

Volunteer Responsibilities

This section explains the main responsibilities of a Volunteer in CSE-VMS and how Volunteer activities connect with the Coordinator and Community Members.

1

Maintain Your Profile

Keep your profile, location and availability accurate and updated.

2

Register Your Skills

Register relevant skills and experience to help the Coordinator match you with community needs.

3

Review Assignments

Review assigned community needs and respond responsibly to each assignment.

4

Support Community Members

Use your skills, knowledge and time to provide appropriate community support.

5

Communicate with the Coordinator

Communicate clearly about assignments, availability and any required coordination.

6

Provide Feedback

Provide useful feedback after completing community service.

Relationship Between Volunteer and Community Member

The Community Member identifies and submits a community need. The Coordinator reviews the need and available Volunteer information such as skills and availability. The Coordinator manages the matching and assignment process. The Volunteer then reviews the assignment, responds to it and provides the required community service. After the service, feedback can be provided to support accountability and improvement.

COMMUNITY MEMBER Identifies and submits a community need.
→
COORDINATOR Reviews needs, skills and availability and manages volunteer assignments.
→
VOLUNTEER Reviews the assignment, responds and provides service.
→
COMMUNITY SUPPORT Volunteer skills are used to address the identified community need.

What the Coordinator Can See About a Volunteer

When a Volunteer creates or updates a profile and registers skills, the information is stored through the Volunteer module and can be used by the Coordinator during volunteer review and matching. The Coordinator can use relevant Volunteer information such as profile details, registered skills, experience level and availability when managing community assignments.

Volunteer Profile

The volunteer creates and maintains a complete profile containing personal information, location, availability, biography, and other relevant details. This information helps the Coordinator understand the volunteer and manage suitable community assignments.

Skills and Experience

The volunteer registers relevant skills and experience in the system. These skills help the Coordinator identify volunteers whose abilities match the needs submitted by Community Members.

Community Support

After receiving an assignment, the volunteer works with the Community Member to provide appropriate support based on the identified community need, while following the assignment information provided through the system.

Assignment Management

The volunteer can review available assignments, see important information about a community need, and respond to assignments through the Volunteer Panel.

Communication

The Volunteer Panel provides a central place for the volunteer to follow notifications and information related to assignments, activities, and communication with the Coordinator.

Feedback and Participation

After providing community support, the volunteer can participate in activities and provide service feedback through the system. This information helps document participation and improve future community services.
